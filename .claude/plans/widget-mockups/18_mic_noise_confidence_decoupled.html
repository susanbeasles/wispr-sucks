The dictation widget with three fully independent signals on the approved red-blue-purple palette. Energy sets bar height. Noise controls motion only: low noise is a smooth rhythmic synchronized wave, high noise is turbulent and desynchronized but not weaker. Confidence controls signal integrity and saturation only: high confidence is dense continuous blocks with no gaps and full saturation, low confidence drops cells and breaks columns into a lossy fragmented signal and desaturates. The microphone reflects confidence alone, so it stays locked and fully red in a noisy room as long as the speaker is understood. Finalization briefly heals the gaps, pops saturation, and locks the mic. Presets include clear speech, mumbling, quiet but clear, noisy but understood, and noisy and lost.
