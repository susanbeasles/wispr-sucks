Two release signals. A confident release fills a clean white ring around the border, holds and blooms, and the mic locks solid. A low-confidence release uses no white: the border goes muted gray, only fills partway, stutters and flickers as it tries to lock, does not bloom, and the mic stays desaturated and wavers, communicating an uncertain commit. Both then power down to black. Controls: presets, energy noise and confidence sliders, speculative-final toggle, finalize, three-second recovery, and confident or unsure release.
